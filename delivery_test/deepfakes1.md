### HELLO, VLADIMIR? IT'S JUST A LITTLE OVERFLIGHT.

### A geopolitical farce in eight acts, featuring one invisible Chinese rocket, one Soviet nuclear warhead, one French First Lady, and a website that sells everything except accountability.

A work of fiction. No rockets, nuclear warheads, or customer-service representatives were consulted in the writing of this sketch.

CAST

EMMANUEL MACRON — President of France. A man who has discovered that being head of state is mostly being asked to apologise for decisions made by people whose names he is not permitted to know.

VLADIMIR PUTIN — President of Russia. Has absolute control over the country, except for its military hardware, the internet, the truth, and his own deepfakes.

BRIGITTE MACRON — Wife of Emmanuel. Proud owner of a Chinese stealth cruise missile purchased on Temu. Has read the reviews. Trusts the reviews more than NATO.

DMITRI PESKOV — Putin’s spokesman. Or a deepfake of Putin’s spokesman. Or a deepfake of a deepfake of Putin’s spokesman. Nobody knows. Neither does he.

THE FRENCH GENERAL — Has a clipboard, three classified folders and no idea what is happening.

CNN — Broadcasting live, because the advertising department has finally found a use for the apocalypse.

THE TEMU CUSTOMER-SERVICE CHATBOT — The only participant who will not panic, because it has no idea what a nuclear war is.
