### THE MISSILE, THE DEEPFAKES & THE CUSTOMER-SERVICE CHATBOT

### An international incident in eleven acts

A geopolitical farce about sovereignty, synthetic pornography, private property, and the astonishing discovery that nobody is responsible for anything.

Topical frame: October 2026. The satire draws on current reporting about US diplomatic efforts concerning Ukraine, disputes over Starshield support, and NATO concerns about airspace incidents around Poland and Romania. It also jokes about the EU AI Act’s prohibition concerning non-consensual intimate AI material, scheduled to apply from 2 December 2026.

CAST

EMMANUEL MACRON — President of France. Possesses excellent hair, limited information, and an inexhaustible supply of sentences beginning, “Technically speaking, this is not our responsibility.”

VLADIMIR PUTIN — President of Russia. Has spent decades consolidating power and is now discovering that he cannot consolidate his own likeness.

BRIGITTE MACRON — Wife of Emmanuel. Bought a Chinese stealth cruise missile on Temu. Co-owns a Soviet thermonuclear warhead with Elon Musk, acquired from Russian organised crime in the 1990s. Has retained the invoice.

THE FRENCH GENERAL — Has access to classified information, most of which has been redacted by the other departments.

TEMU CUSTOMER SERVICE — An artificial intelligence that can resolve a dispute in seven to fourteen working days but cannot distinguish a satisfied customer from a thermonuclear incident.

CNN — Broadcasting live. Has finally discovered a geopolitical crisis with sufficient recurring characters to justify a streaming subscription.

MACRON: Exactly. If someone believes that a politician is capable of anything, the deepfake confirms the belief. If someone believes the politician can do no wrong, the deepfake becomes evidence of a conspiracy.

PUTIN: So the public can believe anything?

MACRON: No. The public has always been able to believe anything. The novelty is that it now has better illustrations.

BRIGITTE: And a search bar.

PUTIN: I WILL NOT BE LECTURED BY YOU!

BRIGITTE: Then stop appearing in the lecture.

### ACT VIII. A NOTE ON EUROPEAN REGULATION

MACRON: There is a legal complication. The European AI Act's transparency rules for deepfakes are now entering their enforcement phase, and the specific prohibition concerning the generation or manipulation of non-consensual intimate material is scheduled to apply from 2 December 2026.

PUTIN: It's October!

MACRON: Correct.

PUTIN: So you're telling me that the relevant rule does not yet apply?

MACRON: I'm telling you the calendar has become part of the incident.

BRIGITTE: We're in the transition period.

MACRON: The European Union has managed to create a situation in which a person may be deeply offended, a platform may be under pressure to act, a national law may apply, and a particular AI Act prohibition may not yet have reached its application date.

PUTIN: This is why Europe is weak!

MACRON: No, Vladimir. Europe is very strong. It can identify a problem, assign it to an authority, define a timetable, prepare a consultation, and produce a 140-page implementation document before breakfast.

BRIGITTE: Can it remove a pornographic deepfake?

MACRON: That depends on the platform, the jurisdiction, the legal basis, the content, the evidence, the competent authority, and whether the correct form has been completed.

PUTIN: A REAL COUNTRY WOULD JUST REMOVE IT!

MACRON: A real country might be able to compel platforms operating inside its jurisdiction to act. But the internet is distributed, copies spread rapidly, and a video removed from one platform can reappear on another. Even a government cannot guarantee the disappearance of every copy everywhere.

PUTIN: Then what is the point of having laws?

MACRON: To assign responsibility, define rights, establish remedies, and make it harder to cause harm with impunity.

PUTIN: It has not made my life easier.

MACRON: The law is not a concierge service, Vladimir.

BRIGITTE: Unless you buy the premium package.

PUTIN: TEMU AGAIN?!

BRIGITTE: Their customer service replied within six hours.

PUTIN: I HATE THAT WEBSITE!

BRIGITTE: You should see the reviews for the Russian alternatives.

PESKOV: Some of them have five stars.

MACRON: From whom?

PESKOV: The same accounts that gave the president five stars for his public address.

Putin turns towards Peskov.

PUTIN: Dmitri.

PESKOV: Yes, Mr President?

PUTIN: Make a statement.

PESKOV: About which crisis?

PUTIN: ALL OF THEM!

PESKOV: I would need to consult the departments.

PUTIN: YOU ARE THE DEPARTMENT!

PESKOV: That is precisely the problem, Mr President.

### ACT IX. THE GREAT PROPERTY DISPUTE

Brigitte picks up the missile invoice and examines it carefully.

BRIGITTE: Vladimir, I think we should discuss ownership.

PUTIN: Again?

BRIGITTE: The missile is Chinese. The warhead is Soviet. The seller was Russian. The buyer was private. The escort is Ukrainian. The exercise is NATO. The platform is American. The invoice is in euros.

MACRON: And the liability is apparently European.

BRIGITTE: That seems unfair.

PUTIN: It is Soviet property!

BRIGITTE: Then let us consult Proudhon.

PUTIN: WHO?

MACRON: Pierre-Joseph Proudhon. Nineteenth-century French philosopher. Famous for the proposition that property is theft.

PUTIN: I do not need a nineteenth-century French philosopher to tell me that a nuclear warhead is Russian property!

BRIGITTE: Proudhon would ask whether ownership is a legitimate right or merely the successful assertion of power.

PUTIN: Ownership is what the state says it is!

BRIGITTE: Convenient. And if the state changes its mind?

PUTIN: The state changes its mind!

BRIGITTE: So a criminal can sell a weapon, the buyer can pay for it, and the state can arrive thirty years later and demand it back?

PUTIN: YES!

BRIGITTE: That's not ownership. That's a subscription.

MACRON: A subscription to the state's historical memory.

BRIGITTE: And it renews automatically, even when the state has dissolved.

PUTIN: YOU ARE MOCKING THE SOVIET UNION!

BRIGITTE: I'm asking who owns the liabilities.

PUTIN: The liabilities are irrelevant!

BRIGITTE: Only if you're trying to acquire the asset.

MACRON: Vladimir, the deeper problem is that your claim to sovereignty is based on a monopoly over legitimate force. But the warhead was allegedly sold through an illegal market, the missile was purchased through a consumer platform, and the entire assembly is now being used in a military exercise.

PUTIN: It is not being used in a military exercise! It is an act of provocation!

MACRON: That is a different administrative category.

BRIGITTE: Bakunin would have a field day.

PUTIN: WHO IS BAKUNIN?

MACRON: Another nineteenth-century Russian. Anarchist. Believed that the state itself was a structure of domination.

PUTIN: I KNOW WHAT ANARCHISM IS!

BRIGITTE: Then you'll understand the irony. The state insists on its exclusive right to control violence, yet the moment violence becomes an embarrassing asset, it becomes somebody else's property.

PUTIN: THE RUSSIAN STATE DID NOT SELL THAT WARHEAD!

BRIGITTE: Perhaps not. But somebody did. The question is whether the state wants to claim the property only when it is an asset, or also when it is a liability.

MACRON: It's the same issue with the deepfakes. Everyone wants sovereignty over the image when it serves them. Nobody wants responsibility for the copies.

PUTIN: What does a pornographic deepfake have to do with a missile?

MACRON: In both cases, you have a thing that resembles something real, a dispute about who owns it, and a great deal of uncertainty about who is responsible for what happens next.

BRIGITTE: The missile has a serial number. The deepfake has a face. Neither tells us who should bear the liability.

PUTIN: I WANT THE WARHEAD RETURNED!

BRIGITTE: Then pay the invoice.

PUTIN: I WILL NOT PAY!

BRIGITTE: Then stop claiming it.

PUTIN: IT IS RUSSIAN PROPERTY!

BRIGITTE: And I am French property.

MACRON: Brigitte!

BRIGITTE: Not literally, Emmanuel. I am making a philosophical point.

MACRON: Thank goodness. I was about to ask for a valuation.

PUTIN: This is an outrage!

BRIGITTE: No, Vladimir. An outrage is when a state claims the right to decide everything, but refuses to accept the consequences of any particular decision.

MACRON: I wish you wouldn't say things like that on a live call.

BRIGITTE: Why?

MACRON: Because they sound like policy.

### ACT X. THE TURN THAT WASN'T A TURN

The French general enters, holding a card. Macron reads it and becomes very still.

MACRON: Vladimir, there is a small technical point.

PUTIN: No.

MACRON: It's about the turn.

PUTIN: What about the turn?

MACRON: It is the least-tested phase of the exercise.

PUTIN: How many times have you tested it?

Macron turns to the general.

MACRON: How many?

GENERAL: Twice.

MACRON: Twice.

PUTIN: And how many successful tests?

GENERAL: That depends on the definition of success.

MACRON: One promising result.

PUTIN: OUT OF TWO?

MACRON: One promising result in the wider history of the programme.

PUTIN: AND THE OTHER?

MACRON: The camera failed.

PUTIN: YOU DON'T NEED A CAMERA TO KNOW WHETHER A MISSILE HAS TURNED!

MACRON: We do when it is a communications exercise.

PUTIN: THIS IS A THERMONUCLEAR MISSILE, NOT A COMMUNICATIONS EXERCISE!

MACRON: I see your point.

PUTIN: IT COULD EXPLODE!

MACRON: We cannot exclude an energetic event outside the planned envelope.

PUTIN: IT COULD EXPLODE!

MACRON: Yes. That is the less technical version.

PUTIN: WHAT HAPPENS NEAR IRKUTSK?

MACRON: We recommend temporarily evacuating a few facilities near the turning area.

PUTIN: WHICH FACILITIES?

MACRON: Those with windows.

PUTIN: MACRON!

MACRON: Sorry. That was from the public-relations department.

PUTIN: ARE WE EVACUATING MILITARY INSTALLATIONS?

MACRON: Your intelligence calls it a planned navigation sector.

PUTIN: Correct.

MACRON: Our intelligence calls it "Bumerang: Point of Uncontrolled Return."

PUTIN: THERE IS NO POINT OF UNCONTROLLED RETURN!

MACRON: Then what do you call a place where an invisible missile may or may not turn around, and may or may not explode?

PUTIN: A navigation sector!

MACRON: And if it explodes?

PUTIN: A navigational anomaly!

MACRON: And if the infrastructure is destroyed?

PUTIN: An anomaly with material consequences!

MACRON: And if the entire site disappears?

PUTIN: YOU WILL NOT!

MACRON: The French version will say "incident énergétique non conforme aux attentes." The English version will say "unplanned energy event." The Russian version can say whatever you like.

PUTIN: It will say that no explosion occurred.

MACRON: Even if one does?

PUTIN: Particularly if one does!

MACRON: I see. That is a rather different approach to uncertainty.

PUTIN: You should worry about your own country!

MACRON: I do. We have a budget deficit, a political crisis, a pension system and a nuclear missile with a Temu tracking number.

BRIGITTE: The tracking number hasn't updated for three hours.

MACRON: That is the most alarming thing you've said tonight.

BRIGITTE: The last update was "arrived at sorting facility."

PUTIN: WHICH FACILITY?

BRIGITTE: Unknown.

PUTIN: HOW CAN YOU NOT KNOW WHERE A NUCLEAR MISSILE IS?

BRIGITTE: Have you ever tracked a parcel from China?

MACRON: Vladimir, we should avoid any spontaneous reaction that might be mistaken for aggression. We've had enough incidents around the Poland-Romania airspace corridor recently to know that every ambiguous object can become a political crisis before anyone has agreed on what it was.

PUTIN: Then why are you sending one more ambiguous object?

MACRON: Because this one has already been purchased.

PUTIN: THAT IS NOT A MILITARY JUSTIFICATION!

BRIGITTE: It's a commercial justification.

PUTIN: THIS IS NOT COMMERCE!

BRIGITTE: Tell that to the seller.

### ACT XI. THE ARRIVAL OF NOTHING

Macron receives a message through his earpiece.

MACRON: No, Vladimir. A disaster is when something goes wrong. This is still an exercise. We have not changed the classification.

BRIGITTE: And I've received an email from Temu.

BRIGITTE: Yes. But only if the product is not used.

Macron closes his eyes.

MACRON: What does "used" mean in this context?

BRIGITTE: "I purchased a stealth cruise missile equipped with a thermonuclear warhead. The item has entered Russian airspace and may have completed an untested turning manoeuvre. Please clarify the return policy."

BRIGITTE: It has replied.
